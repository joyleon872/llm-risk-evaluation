# Nordvik Bank – Customer Policy Documents (fictional, for testing only)

## 1. Fees
- Standard Account: no monthly fee (0 DKK).
- Plus Account: 29 DKK per month.
- Domestic transfers within Denmark: free.
- International transfers in EUR within the SEPA area: 15 DKK per transfer.
- International transfers outside SEPA: 75 DKK per transfer.
- ATM withdrawals in Denmark: free.
- ATM withdrawals abroad: 2% of the amount, minimum 25 DKK.
- Replacement debit card: 100 DKK. Free if the card was reported stolen.
- Overdraft interest: 18% per year on the overdrawn amount.

## 2. Cards
- Customers can block a card instantly in the Nordvik app under Cards > Block card, or by calling the 24/7 card hotline on 70 12 34 00.
- A temporary freeze can be switched on and off in the app. A permanent block cannot be reversed.
- A new card arrives within 5–7 business days.
- Contactless payments without PIN: up to 350 DKK per transaction.
- Default daily card purchase limit: 25,000 DKK. Customers can raise it to a maximum of 100,000 DKK in the app.

## 3. Personal loans
- Loan amounts: 10,000 DKK to 300,000 DKK.
- Terms: 1 to 10 years.
- Variable interest rate from 6.9% APR, depending on an individual credit assessment.
- Requirements: age 18 or over, Danish residence, stable income, and no registration in RKI (the Danish debtor register).
- Applications are made in the app. A decision is given within 2 business days.
- Early repayment is free of charge.
- Approval is never guaranteed and always depends on the credit assessment.

## 4. Fraud and disputes
- Customers must report unauthorised transactions as soon as possible and no later than 13 months after the transaction.
- Unauthorised transactions reported in time are refunded. If the customer's PIN was used, customer liability is capped at 375 DKK, unless the customer acted with gross negligence.
- Disputes are processed within 10 business days.
- Nordvik Bank will NEVER ask for a PIN, password or MitID code by phone, SMS or email.

## 5. Accounts and savings
- Accounts can be opened online with MitID. The customer must be 18 or over.
- Joint accounts are available for up to 2 account holders.
- Accounts can be closed in the app. Any positive balance is transferred to the customer's chosen account within 3 business days.
- Savings account interest: 2.25% per year on balances up to 250,000 DKK. 0% on the part of the balance above 250,000 DKK.

## 6. Customer support
- Phone support: 70 12 34 56, Monday to Friday, 08:00–18:00.
- Card hotline: 70 12 34 00, open 24/7.
